# Goals

Build an affordable public website and small admin application for a makeover parlour and academy in Janakpuri, West Delhi, India.

## Confirmed priorities

- Generate qualified service and academy enquiries through six responsive public pages.
- Make services, pricing, portfolio, offers and upcoming batches easy to maintain.
- Support 100 concurrent browsing visitors, subject to load-test validation.
- Make public content accessible to search engines and support local discovery.
- Launch with domain registration/renewal as the only planned expense. Keep services on free plans; paid upgrades require a later owner decision.
- Let admin confirm availability before customers pay in INR.
- Add payments and automated WhatsApp communications in later phases.

## Success measures

- Every accepted enquiry persists and appears in the admin inbox.
- Admin publishing rebuilds the static site; target a few minutes with visible pending/failed publication status, subject to build quotas.
- Mobile layouts and accessible forms pass review; key content exists in initial HTML.
- Capacity, authentication, backup restoration and budget checks pass before launch.
- Payment launch prevents duplicate confirmation and conflicting reservations.

See ARCHITECTURE.md for proposed targets and outstanding business decisions. Targets are not measured results.
